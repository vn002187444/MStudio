## 2023-10-27 - [Concurrent Promises in Recommendations]
**Learning:** In the initial implementation, data was fetched in two sequential `Promise.all` blocks because the second block depended on the `profile` fetched in the first block. However, the other queries in the first block (`freshIds`, `trending`, `related`) did not need to block the execution of the queries in the second block (`followedIds`, `hiddenArticleIds`). We can execute these queries concurrently to avoid sequential blocking. However, we must be careful not to introduce unhandled promise rejections by starting background promises and not `await`ing them immediately if another earlier promise throws. A safer way is chaining dependencies within a single `Promise.all`.
**Action:** Unrolled the sequential `Promise.all` blocks. Chained `followedIdsPromise` and `hiddenArticleIdsPromise` onto `profilePromise.then()` so that they evaluate sequentially but participate in a single unified `Promise.all` alongside `freshIds`, `trending`, and `related`. This guarantees all errors are caught and handled by the top-level `Promise.all`.
